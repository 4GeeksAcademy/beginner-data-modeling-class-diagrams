# Welcome: How This Practice Works

Spanish version: [README.es.md](./README.es.md)

## What you will do

You will practice **data modeling** with **UML class diagrams** for a **digital library**. Each step adds one concept to the same diagram in [diagram.4geeks.com](https://diagram.4geeks.com/).

## Where you work

- **Diagram tool:** [diagram.4geeks.com](https://diagram.4geeks.com/) — create one diagram and keep extending it from step 01 through 10.
- **Instructions:** This LearnPack repository — **README files only**. There are no `app.js`, tests, or answer files to edit here.
- **Optional:** Export PNG from diagram.4geeks.com for your own notes. You do **not** upload files for this practice.

## How LearnPack behaves

- **No auto-grading:** Steps are not tested. Mark the practice complete when you finish reviewing your work.
- **No delivery:** Nothing is submitted to the platform.
- **Navigation:** You can open any step in any order. Steps are written in a logical sequence; later steps assume earlier modeling work.

## Self-check at the end

When you finish step **09**, open step **10 - Final Model**. It includes a reference **Mermaid `classDiagram`** you can compare with your diagram in diagram.4geeks.com (layout and naming may differ; focus on entities, types, and relationships).

## Before step 01

1. Open [diagram.4geeks.com](https://diagram.4geeks.com/) and start a new class diagram.
2. Continue to **01 - First Entity**.
